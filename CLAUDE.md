# Probate Pipeline

Private working repo for a probate real-estate wholesaling pipeline covering Franklin County OH, Shelby County TN, and Philadelphia PA (research-only).

- The skill lives in `.claude/skills/probate-wholesale-deal/`. For the scheduled daily run, follow `.claude/skills/probate-wholesale-deal/references/daily-run.md` exactly.
- Settings: `config.yaml`. Pipeline: `pipeline/deal-tracker.csv`. Per-lead files: `leads/<lead_id>/`. Daily reports: `reports/`. State legal notes: `legal/`.
- Never contact executors, heirs, attorneys, buyers or courts. Outreach is drafts only for the owner to approve.
- This repo contains personal information; keep it private and don't copy it elsewhere.
- Nothing here is legal or tax advice; legal files list what to confirm with a local attorney or CPA.
