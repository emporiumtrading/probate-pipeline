How we work

We find estates through public probate court records and write to the person the court has appointed, or to their attorney. If you'd like an offer, we look at the home, explain how we arrived at our price in writing, and give you time to talk it over with your attorney and family.

We may buy the home ourselves, or we may arrange for another investor to buy it under our contract. We tell you which in writing before you sign anything, along with any disclosures your state requires.

We are not real-estate agents or attorneys and we don't give legal or tax advice. Please talk to the estate's attorney before you sign.
