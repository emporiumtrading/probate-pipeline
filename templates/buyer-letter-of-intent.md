# Buyer Letter of Intent (non-binding) — DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW

> Draft prepared by the pipeline for review by a licensed attorney in each state before use. Not legal advice.
> Sent only after the market's attorney has approved it (it is part of that market's attorney packet), through the company's e-signature service.

**From:** {buyer_legal_name} ("Buyer"), {buyer_entity_state} {buyer_entity_type}
**To:** {company_legal_name} ("Company"), {company_mailing_address}
**Date:** {date}

## 1. Purpose
Buyer wishes to be offered residential properties that Company has under contract, so that Buyer can decide quickly whether to purchase them. This letter records what Buyer is looking for and how Buyer buys. **It is non-binding:** neither party is obliged to offer, buy or sell any property because of it, except for section 6, which both parties agree to follow.

## 2. What Buyer buys
- Areas (counties, cities or ZIP codes): {areas}
- Property types: {property_types}
- Price range: {price_min} to {price_max}
- Rehab level accepted: {rehab_level}
- Strategy: {strategy}

## 3. Price and terms Buyer generally accepts
- Buyer generally pays up to {max_pct_arv}% of after-repair value, less repairs, for properties that fit section 2.
- Buyer accepts an assignment fee (or double-close spread) of up to {max_assignment_fee} per property, shown on the settlement statement.
- Buyer is prepared to deposit earnest money of {emd_offered} with the title company within {emd_days} business days of signing an assignment agreement.
- Buyer can close within {close_days} days of signing, as-is, paying cash or using the funding described in section 4.

## 4. Proof of funds
Buyer has provided, or will provide on request, proof of funds or a lender commitment dated within the last 90 days: {proof_of_funds}. Company keeps it confidential and uses it only to decide which properties to offer.

## 5. How properties are offered
When Company has a property under contract that fits section 2, it may send Buyer a summary. Any purchase happens only through a separate written assignment agreement (or purchase agreement) signed by both parties, closed through a title company or closing attorney, with earnest money held in that company's escrow account. Company never asks Buyer to wire money to anyone but the title company's escrow, and Buyer confirms any wiring instructions by phone at the number on the title company's official website.

## 6. Conduct (agreed by both parties)
- Buyer will not contact the seller, the estate, its personal representative, heirs or their attorneys about a property Company offers, and will not try to buy it directly, for {non_contact_months} months after receiving its details, unless Company agrees in writing.
- Buyer will keep property details Company shares confidential and use them only to evaluate the purchase.
- Either party may end this letter at any time by email. Buyer may ask at any time to receive no further offers.

## 7. General
This letter is governed by the laws of {governing_state}. It does not create a partnership, agency or brokerage relationship. Company is not acting as a real-estate broker or agent for Buyer.
