## How the workers performed
Every branch ended with a written rule followed: no pressure, no guessing, nothing outside a limit without the Exceptions Desk, and every stop honored. Five situations had only partial rules before this rerun. They are now written into the workers' files:
1. **Occupant still in the house at the walk-through.** Transaction Coordinator: no closing, moving help held, written extension, removal is the PR's court matter.
2. **No buyer at our price.** Dispositions: widen the same day, renegotiate honestly inside the inspection period with documents, or cancel with deposits returned.
3. **Buyer backs out.** Dispositions: backup buyer the same day, deposit per the agreement, red flag recorded.
4. **Surviving spouse.** Heir & Title Tracer: check every petition and record, apply the state rule, add the spouse as a signer.
5. **Nurture and lost-to-listing.** Communications: one letter only on a court sale step, one check-in only when a listing expires.

Plus: Contract Desk now puts an inspection period of at least 10 days in every purchase agreement, explained plainly and never used to hold a family.
