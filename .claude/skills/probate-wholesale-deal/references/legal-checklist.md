# Legal checklist (verify for the property's state)

This is a list of questions, not answers. Laws change; confirm each one with a local real-estate attorney, the state real-estate commission's website, or the title company.

## Wholesaling
1. Does the state require a real-estate license to market or assign a purchase contract? Is there a "pattern of transactions" limit (e.g., a number of deals per year before a license is required)?
2. Does the state require written disclosure to the seller that you intend to assign or that you're not buying as an end user? To the end buyer that you hold only an equitable interest?
3. Is there a registration or bond requirement for wholesalers?
4. Are there limits on advertising a property you don't own (marketing the house vs. marketing the contract)?
5. Does the seller get a cancellation/rescission period by law?
6. Do any cities/counties add their own rules?

## Probate
1. Who is the personal representative and have letters been issued?
2. Does the representative have full authority to sell, or is court approval/confirmation required? (Look for "independent" vs. "supervised" administration.)
3. If court confirmation applies, can others overbid at the hearing? How long does it usually take locally?
4. Are all heirs/devisees required to consent or receive notice?
5. Any creditor claim period that must run before closing?
6. Is there a Medicaid estate recovery claim or other state claim?

## Marketing & contact
1. Lists scrubbed against the National DNC Registry and state DNC lists?
2. Consent in place for any texting or auto-dialed calls (TCPA)?
3. Any state rules on soliciting recently bereaved families?

## Safe defaults if unsure
- Partner with or become a licensed agent.
- Disclose assignment intent in writing in the contract and in conversation.
- Use an attorney-reviewed, state-specific contract.
- Close through a title company or attorney that knows assignments.

## Contract (ask the attorney to review)
1. Is the purchase agreement assignable, and is the assignment disclosure worded to meet state rules?
2. Inspection/due-diligence exit: how long, and what happens to earnest money?
3. Court-approval contingency and what happens at an overbid hearing?
4. Remedies if you fail to close (liquidated damages vs. specific performance)?
5. Access for showing end buyers; personal property left in the home?
6. Is recording a memorandum of contract allowed or wise here? (It can cloud title.)
7. Separate assignment agreement with the end buyer: non-refundable deposit, non-circumvention.

## Business & tax (ask a CPA)
1. Entity: operate as an LLC or other entity? Insurance (general liability; E&O if licensed)?
2. How are assignment fees taxed for you (typically ordinary business income; self-employment tax may apply)?
3. Estimated quarterly tax payments?
4. Deductible costs to track: marketing, skip tracing, data tools, mileage, earnest money lost.
5. Will the title company issue a 1099 for your fee?
