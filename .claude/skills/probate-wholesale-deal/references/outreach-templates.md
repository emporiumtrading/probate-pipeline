# Outreach templates

Adapt to the person and state. Keep them truthful, short, and pressure-free. Always say you are an investor and may assign the contract.

## Letter to personal representative
Dear [Name],

I'm sorry for the loss of [Decedent's name]. I know handling an estate is a lot of work.

My name is [Your name]. I'm a local real-estate investor. I buy houses as-is for cash, and I may assign my purchase contract to another investor. If selling [address] would help settle the estate, I can:
- buy it as-is with no repairs or cleanup,
- cover normal closing costs at a local title company,
- set a closing date that fits the probate timeline.

If now isn't the right time, no problem. You can reach me at [phone/email]. If you'd prefer not to hear from me again, just let me know and I'll remove you from my list.

Sincerely,
[Name, company, license # if any]

## Call opener
"Hi, is this [Name]? My name is [Your name]. I'm a local real-estate investor. I'm calling about the property at [address] in [Decedent]'s estate. First, I'm sorry for your loss. Is now an OK time for a quick question, or should I call back?"

If yes: "Are you planning to keep the house, list it with an agent, or would an as-is cash sale be helpful? … I'll be upfront: I'm an investor, and I may assign the contract to another buyer."

If no: thank them, offer your number, and note "do not contact" if asked.

## To the estate's attorney
"I'm a local investor interested in purchasing [address] from the [Decedent] estate, as-is, cash, with a closing timed to the court's schedule. I disclose up front that I may assign the contract. Could you share whether the representative is open to offers and whether court confirmation will be required?"

## Deal sheet for cash buyers
- Address, beds/baths/sqft, year built
- Price, closing date, earnest money required
- ARV with 3–5 sold comps (addresses, dates, prices)
- Repair estimate and photos
- Access instructions
- Note: "Contract assignment. Probate sale; court approval [required/not required]."
