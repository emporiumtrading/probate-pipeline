    Before any wire

    1. Wire only to the title company's escrow account, never to a seller, heir, attorney or buyer.
    2. Look up the title company's phone number on its official website yourself. Don't use a number from an email, text or PDF.
    3. Call that number and confirm the account and amount with a person before sending.
    4. Treat any change of wiring instructions as fraud until confirmed the same way.
    5. After wiring, mark it I wired it above and ask the title company for a receipt; the Bookkeeper records it as earnest money out.

    How the numbers are worked out

    Cash-buyer max = after-repair value × 70% − repairs. Max offer = cash-buyer max − $10,000 fee. Target price = the Negotiator's aim, normally at or below the max offer; where a legal price floor is higher, the fee shrinks. Potential earnings = cash-buyer max − target price. Every figure is an estimate from public records and comps, checked by automated QA before publishing. Not legal or tax advice.
