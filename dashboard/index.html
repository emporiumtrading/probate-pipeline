    How the numbers are worked out

    Cash-buyer max = after-repair value × 70% − repairs. Max offer = cash-buyer max − $10,000 fee. Target price = the Negotiator's aim, normally at or below max offer; where a legal price floor is higher, the fee shrinks. Potential earnings = cash-buyer max − target price. All figures are estimates from public records and comps, checked by automated QA before publishing. Not legal or tax advice.

    What still stands between the pipeline and running on its own

    Every "missing" row is something only you can provide. Fill in config.yaml (business and channels), send each market's attorney packet, then record approvals. Signatures, wire confirmations and calls always stay with you.
