## 1. Wholesaling, licensing, disclosures, cancellation
| # | Rule | Source | Checked | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | **Iowa Code § 543B.6A (2024 Acts ch. 1040 §1).** "Wholesaling" = entering an assignable contract with an owner of residential property (1–4 units) to market and sell the equitable interest. **"A person must be licensed as a real estate broker ... or be represented by a real estate broker licensed under this chapter, to engage in wholesaling."** | https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2025/543B.6A.pdf | 2026-11-19 | VERIFIED |
| 1.2 | Written disclosure to **all parties before** the purchase contract (and before conveying the equitable interest): legal identities of all parties; explanation of wholesaling, incl. that the wholesaler holds only an equitable interest and may not be able to convey title. A copy of the executed **agency agreement** (wholesaler–broker) must also go to all parties beforehand. | same, sub. 3(a)–(b) | 2026-11-19 | VERIFIED |
| 1.3 | **Cancellation:** if the disclosure is missing, seller or buyer may cancel any time before closing without penalty and **keep the wholesaler's earnest money**, notwithstanding the contract. | same, 3(c) | 2026-11-19 | VERIFIED |
| 1.4 | **Penalty:** civil penalty up to the greater of **$10,000 or 10% of the sale price** per property. | same, sub. 4 | 2026-11-19 | VERIFIED |
