## 1. Wholesaling, licensing, required disclosures, cancellation

**Bottom line: no Indiana license is required just to put a home under contract and assign it.** There is no wholesaler license or registration. Since 2024, Indiana requires a **disclosure in every solicitation** and gives the homeowner a **2-day rescission right** if the disclosure is defective. **Market status: outreach allowed (drafts only under pipeline settings).**

## Required contract / solicitation disclosures

**In every letter, email, postcard and spoken script (IC 32-21-16.5-4), legible and in plain sight:**

> **This solicitation is not from a licensed real estate professional.** Solicitor: [OUR LEGAL NAME]. Expected purchaser: [OUR LEGAL NAME and/or assigns / END BUYER LEGAL NAME if known].

**Before signing (IC 32-21-16.5-5):** written note to the PR stating whether anything in that disclosure changed (e.g. "The expected purchaser is now [name]" or "No change").

Also in the purchase agreement (attorney to draft): "and/or assigns"; plain written statement that we are an investor who may assign the contract for a fee; PR signs "as Personal Representative of the Estate of ___, Cause No. 49D08-____"; for ES estates, court-approval contingency; inspection/exit period; earnest money held by title; as-is; personal property; access for showings; no recorded memorandum without attorney approval. Separate assignment agreement with the end buyer.
