## What we do
We find estates in probate that own real property, write to the personal representative (or the estate's attorney, when one is listed), and offer to buy the property as-is for cash with no fees or repairs for the estate. We sign a purchase contract with the estate, then either assign that contract to a vetted cash buyer for a fee or buy and resell at a simultaneous closing. The title company holds all earnest money and handles closing. We disclose our intent to assign in writing, we never call or text without the legal basis to do so, and we honor every stop request the same day.

## Blocking issues (stop until answered)

1. **Attorney confirms the assignment lane is not brokerage** under 475.01/475.42 for our exact model (we sign as principal buyer with "and/or assigns", market only the contract to a private buyer list, collect fee via assignment at closing). Felony exposure if wrong.
2. **No public advertising of the house** (photos, address, beds/baths on public sites/social) — contract marketing to a private, vetted buyer list only.
3. **Homestead check per lead:** protected homestead → all heirs (and any spouse life-estate holder) sign; non-homestead intestate → court authorization/confirmation contingency.
4. **Flood disclosure (689.302)** must be given by the seller at or before contract — confirm who signs it in an estate sale and include it in the package.
5. **No fee splits** with anyone until the attorney approves.
6. **Phone/text:** none until FTSA/DNC process is set up.

## Required contract disclosures

No statutory wholesaler form in Florida (as of 2026-11-19). Pipeline-required wording (attorney to approve), on the first page of the purchase agreement and in every letter:

> **"Buyer is a real estate investor purchasing for its own account and is not a licensed real estate broker or agent and does not represent the Seller. Buyer may assign this contract to another buyer before closing and may receive a fee from that buyer. Seller is encouraged to consult an attorney before signing."**

Also: s. 689.302 Flood Disclosure form signed by seller at/before contract; "and/or assigns"; court authorization/confirmation contingency (733.613(1)) or homestead-heir signature requirement; PR signs "as Personal Representative of the Estate of ___, Hillsborough County Case No. ___"; inspection/exit period; small earnest money held by a Florida title agent; as-is; personal-property clause; access for buyer walkthroughs; no recorded memorandum without attorney sign-off. Separate assignment agreement with end buyer (non-refundable deposit, non-circumvention).
