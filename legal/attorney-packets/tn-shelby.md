## What we do
We find estates in probate that own real property, write to the personal representative (or the estate's attorney, when one is listed), and offer to buy the property as-is for cash with no fees or repairs for the estate. We sign a purchase contract with the estate, then either assign that contract to a vetted cash buyer for a fee or buy and resell at a simultaneous closing. The title company holds all earnest money and handles closing. We disclose our intent to assign in writing, we never call or text without the legal basis to do so, and we honor every stop request the same day.

## Blocking issues (stop until answered)

1. **Contract must contain the 66-4-403 disclosures in bold, large print, inside the written agreement, signed before execution** (intent to market our equitable interest). No TN contract without the attorney-approved clause.
2. **3-business-day pre-assignment notice to the seller** stating the effective date of the assignment must be built into the closing calendar — an assignment cannot take effect sooner. Transaction Coordinator must track this.
3. **Written disclosure to every end buyer/assignee** of the nature of our equitable interest (in the assignment agreement).
4. **Who can sign:** confirm per lead whether the will grants a power of sale; if not, all heirs must sign or a court-approved sale is needed. A PR-only contract without authority is blocked.
5. **No public advertising of the house** without a license or licensed partner; **no fee splits** until attorney approval.
6. **TN Do-Not-Call/Text Register** access/scrub must be in place before any phone or text outreach draft is approved.

## Required contract disclosures

The statute prescribes **content, not exact wording** (T.C.A. § 66-4-403, VERIFIED from enacted SB 909 text). Draft language for the attorney to review — print **in bold, large font, in the purchase agreement itself**:

> **SELLER DISCLOSURE (T.C.A. § 66-4-403): Buyer intends to market and may assign Buyer's equitable interest in this contract to another purchaser for a price higher than the price Buyer is paying Seller. "Equitable interest" means Buyer's right to benefit or profit from the property after this contract is signed and before legal title transfers. If Buyer assigns this contract, Buyer will notify Seller in writing of the effective date of the assignment at least three (3) business days before that date.**

In the assignment agreement with the end buyer:

> **ASSIGNEE DISCLOSURE (T.C.A. § 66-4-403): Assignor does not own the property. Assignor holds only an equitable interest as buyer under a purchase contract dated ____ with ____ (Seller), which is being assigned to you for a fee/price higher than Assignor's contract price.**

Plus: separate written 3-business-day assignment notice to seller (keep proof of delivery); "and/or assigns"; probate authority/court-approval contingency; inspection/exit and earnest-money terms; as-is; access for showings; personal property; fiduciary/heir signature blocks; no recorded memorandum without attorney sign-off.
