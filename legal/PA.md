## 4. Tax & entity (questions for a CPA/attorney — UNVERIFIED)

- **Realty transfer tax (state 1% + Philadelphia ~3.278%, verify rates):** PA Dept. of Revenue letter ruling RTT-08-002 says assignment documents are not taxable in themselves, but Bulletin 2008-01 indicates an assignment can trigger **additional** transfer tax unless the assignor is released (novation). Double closes typically pay transfer tax **twice**. In Philadelphia this can erase the spread. Sources: https://www.revenue.pa.gov/TaxLawPoliciesBulletinsNotices/LetterRulings/RTT/Documents/rtt-08-002.pdf ; https://www.phila.gov/media/20151208132337/Real-Estate-Transfer-Tax-Regulations.pdf (not read in full).
- Philadelphia **BIRT** (Business Income & Receipts Tax) and **Net Profits Tax** on fees; Commercial Activity License — required anyway for the wholesaler license.
- PA personal income tax on net business profits; federal ordinary income + SE tax.
- Licensing costs, E&O insurance (if licensed), $1M GL policy for the Philadelphia license.

## Required contract disclosures

From RELRA § 610(e) as added by Act 52 (VERIFIED 2026-11-19). Must appear **prominently** in the sales agreement, in a manner the State Real Estate Commission may set by regulation (none found yet). Draft for attorney review (content tracks statute):

> **WHOLESALE TRANSACTION DISCLOSURE (63 P.S. § 455.610)**
> **1. This agreement is for a wholesale transaction in which the licensee intends to assign, sell or otherwise transfer the interest for a fee, commission or other valuable monetary consideration without having taken title as the owner of record of the interest.**
> **2. You have the right to obtain an appraisal of the property from a real estate appraiser certified under section 3 of the Real Estate Appraisers Certification Act, to consult with a licensee not affiliated with the licensee's broker, or to seek legal counsel before or after entering into this agreement.**
> **3. You have the right to cancel this agreement until midnight of the 30th day after the date you signed it, or until conveyance, whichever occurs first, by certified return receipt mail or by any other bona fide means of delivery, including electronic delivery or personal delivery, provided that you obtain a receipt.**
> **4. Within ten business days after receipt of a notice of cancellation, all payments of any kind made by you will be refunded to you.**

Also (Philadelphia, UNVERIFIED): deliver the City's **Homeowner Bill of Rights** at least 3 days before signing; show wholesaler license number. Plus the usual: PR signature "as Executor/Administrator of the Estate of ___," specific devisee joinder if applicable, bond/court-order contingency (§§ 3351, 3353), inheritance-tax clearance, inspection/exit terms, no memorandum recorded without attorney sign-off.
