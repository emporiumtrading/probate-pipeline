# Louisiana (LA) — Legal Issue List for Succession Wholesaling (market: East Baton Rouge Parish / Baton Rouge)

> **NOT LEGAL OR TAX ADVICE.** AI-compiled from public sources to brief a Louisiana attorney. Louisiana is a civil-law state: "probate" = **succession**. VERIFIED = read on the official page on the date shown; UNVERIFIED = confirm with an attorney. Re-check every 30 days.

**Bottom line: no wholesaler license, but NEW Act 807 (2026) rules: written disclosures, mandatory LREC cancellation notice, rescission period, 1% escrowed deposit. `outreach_allowed: true` (drafts only).**

## 1. Wholesaling, licensing, disclosures, cancellation

| 1.1 | **Act 807 of 2026 (HB 468), effective 2026-08-01; adds R.S. 37:1431(35)–(37) and R.S. 37:1448.5.** Defines residential real property, wholesaler, wholesaling. | 1.4 | Missing disclosure/any violation → contract **voidable by seller any time before transfer of title**; seller keeps the deposit (except termination inside the rescission period → deposit back to wholesaler). **Deposit ≥ 1% of price, held in escrow** (LA federally insured institution) or seller's account. | same | 2026-11-19 | VERIFIED (digest) |
| 1.5 | Enforcement: unfair trade practice (AG) **and** violation of the Real Estate License Law (LREC); civil penalty up to **$5,000 per violation**. No separate wholesaler license created. | same | 2026-11-19 | VERIFIED (digest) |

## 2. Succession sale authority
| # | Rule | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | **Independent administration** (La. C.C.P. arts. 3396–3396.20): independent executor/administrator may sell without court approval. | La. C.C.P. | UNVERIFIED |
| 2.2 | Otherwise a **private sale needs a court order after published notice** (C.C.P. arts. 3281–3282), with a window for heirs/creditors to oppose. | La. C.C.P. | UNVERIFIED |
| 2.3 | Many successions end in a **judgment of possession** with no administration — then **all heirs/legatees sign**; surviving spouse often holds a **usufruct** over the decedent's community share (C.C. art. 890) and must sign too. | La. Civil Code | UNVERIFIED |
