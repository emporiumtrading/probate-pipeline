## Blocking issues
1. Every AL contract needs the 8-42-2 written disclosures: intent-to-market (before any marketing), assignee notice, and **3-business-day pre-assignment notice** to the seller. Missing one = misdemeanor + 3x fee.
2. Sale authority per lead (will power vs. court order).

## Required contract disclosures
Separate signed page (attorney to word): "Buyer is acquiring an equitable interest under this contract and intends to market and may assign that interest to a third party for a fee. Buyer will give Seller written notice of the effective date of any assignment at least three business days before it takes effect." Plus a written disclosure to the assignee of the nature of the equitable interest.
