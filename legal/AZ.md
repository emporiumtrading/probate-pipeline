## Required contract disclosures
§ 44-5101 has **no statutory form text**. Suggested (attorney to approve), in bold on a separate page signed and dated before the purchase contract:

> **"WHOLESALE BUYER DISCLOSURE (A.R.S. § 44-5101). [Buyer] is a wholesale buyer. [Buyer] is entering into this purchase contract with the intent to assign the contract to another buyer for a fee and may not close on the purchase itself. [Buyer] does not represent the Seller. Seller may seek legal or professional advice before signing."**

To the end buyer (if we ever act as wholesale seller): disclose we are a wholesale seller, hold only an equitable interest, and may be unable to convey title.

Purchase agreement (attorney to draft): "and/or assigns"; PR signs "as Personal Representative of the Estate of ___, Maricopa County Superior Court No. PB____"; copy of letters (and any restrictions) as a condition; inspection/exit period; small earnest money held by title; as-is; personal-property clause; access for showings; no recorded memorandum without attorney sign-off. Separate assignment agreement with the end buyer.
