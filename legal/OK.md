# Oklahoma (OK) — Legal Issue List for Probate Wholesaling (market: Oklahoma County / Oklahoma City)

**Bottom line: no license needed to wholesale **unless you publicly market the equitable interest**; strict disclosures + 2-business-day cancellation. `outreach_allowed: true` (drafts only).**

## 1. Wholesaling, licensing, disclosures, cancellation
| # | Rule | Source | Checked | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | **59 O.S. § 858-102(20)** (SB 1075, 2025): "wholesaler" = anyone in the business of securing/negotiating/facilitating sale of residential real estate primarily to transfer/assign/sell their equitable interest for profit — **includes double closings**. | OREC Code & Rules Book (Nov 1 2026) https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/orec/documents/meetings/Nov%201%202026%20OREC%20Code%20and%20Rules%20Book.pdf | 2026-11-19 | VERIFIED |
| 1.2 | **59 O.S. § 858-301:** it is acting as a licensee (license required) to **publicly market for sale an equitable interest** in a purchase contract. Private assignment is not itself licensed. | same | 2026-11-19 | VERIFIED |
| 1.3 | **59 O.S. § 858-314:** before any contract, disclose in writing the intent to assign/sell the equitable interest for a higher price; prominent "seek legal advice" notice; disclose the **2-business-day** cancellation right. Contract must list wholesaler name/address/phone, property address, total consideration, payment terms, and the statutory **"NOTICE REQUIRED BY OKLAHOMA LAW"** box (≥12-pt bold) next to the seller signature; attach OREC notice-of-cancellation form (01-01-2026). No deed or other document may be signed until the cancel period ends. | same | 2026-11-19 | VERIFIED |
| 1.4 | Missing disclosures → contract **invalid and unenforceable by the wholesaler**, homeowner gets the earnest money; homeowner may terminate any time for noncompliance. Earnest money must be in an **Oklahoma escrow account** at a federally insured institution. Wholesaler may not act as advisor, claim licenses it lacks, or **place any lien/cloud on title**. | same | 2026-11-19 | VERIFIED |

## 3. Contact (TCPA / DNC)
- Federal TCPA/National DNC apply. **Oklahoma Telephone Solicitation Act (15 O.S. § 775C.1 et seq., 2022)**: consent for automated calls/texts, hours and frequency limits, private right of action. UNVERIFIED.

## Blocking issues
1. No public marketing (social, MLS, websites) of the contract without an OK license — private buyers list only. 2. § 858-314 contract form + OREC cancellation form; nothing signed until the 2 business days pass. 3. 90% appraisal floor + court confirmation vs. MAO.
