## 1. Wholesaling, licensing, disclosures, cancellation
| # | Rule | Source | Checked | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | **RSMo § 407.3600 (2026 H.B. 2636 / S.B. 973), effective 2026-08-28.** "Wholesaler" = person who for a fee/consideration enters a purchase contract for residential real property (1–4 units) as grantee and assigns/novates it (or as grantor without legal title). Exempt: assignment to relatives within 3rd degree, or affiliates under common control. | https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=407.3600 | 2026-11-19 | VERIFIED |
| 1.2 | **Disclosure at least 14 calendar days before entering into the contract**: separate document, boldface, ≥12-pt, statutory wording (wholesaler acts for itself; owner should get legal advice; may assign without owner consent; may charge buyer a fee; price may be below market). Owner and wholesaler sign and date it. **No binding contract until signed.** | same, subs. 2–3 | 2026-11-19 | VERIFIED |
| 1.3 | **Cancellation:** if not disclosed, owner may cancel any time before close of escrow without penalty; escrow agent returns earnest money **to the owner** within 30 days. Not waivable. | same, subs. 4–5 | 2026-11-19 | VERIFIED |
| 1.4 | **Enforcement:** unlawful practice under the Merchandising Practices Act; private action; AG civil action (damages, injunction, attorney fees). | same, subs. 6–7 | 2026-11-19 | VERIFIED |
| 1.5 | No license required to assign one's own contract (Missouri REALTORS commentary). ## Blocking issues
1. § 407.3600 disclosure form, attorney-approved, signed **≥14 days before** any purchase contract. 2. Who is the "record owner" in an estate (PR vs. heirs)? 3. Independent vs. supervised administration on each lead.

## Required contract disclosures
Statutory § 407.3600 form (separate page, bold, ≥12-pt), signed and dated by owner and wholesaler ≥14 days before contract; "and/or assigns"; PR signs in fiduciary capacity; court-approval contingency if supervised.
